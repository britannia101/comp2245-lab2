Learning to Code Without Losing My Mind

October 2, 2026 by Britannia Bucknall

Learning to code has definitely been an experience. At first, even simple programs could feel confusing because one small mistake could stop everything from working. As I continued practicing, I started understanding how different parts of a program work together and how to find problems in my code.

University has introduced me to different areas of computing, including programming, networking and web development. I still run into errors that make me question everything, but solving them is one of the most satisfying parts of learning. Every assignment gives me another opportunity to improve my skills and become more confident with technology.

Why I Crochet When My Brain Needs a Break

October 4, 2026 by Britannia Bucknall

Spending hours studying or working on code can get exhausting, so crocheting is one of the things I enjoy doing when I need a break. It gives me something creative to focus on without having to stare at another error message or figure out why my program suddenly decided not to work.

I like seeing a project slowly come together from just yarn and an idea. Whether I am working on something simple or learning a new pattern, crocheting gives me a chance to slow down and reset. Having hobbies outside of technology helps me come back to my schoolwork feeling more focused.
